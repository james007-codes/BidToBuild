PATIENT CARE SUMMARY
Record ID: CF-2024-0417
Facility: Northside General Hospital
Prepared: 14 November 2024

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 - PATIENT BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Patient is a 58-year-old male presenting with a three-week history of
intermittent chest discomfort on exertion. He reports the discomfort
resolves within five minutes of rest.

Relevant history: type 2 diabetes (diagnosed 2016), hyperlipidaemia,
and a 20 pack-year smoking history with cessation in 2019. No prior
cardiac procedures. Family history is significant for myocardial
infarction in his father at age 61.

The patient is a resident of Pune and was travelling for work at the
time of presentation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 - INITIAL ASSESSMENT (04 November 2024)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

On arrival, blood pressure was 148/92 mmHg with a regular pulse of
78 beats per minute. The patient was afebrile and fully oriented.

Laboratory findings:
  - Fasting glucose        142 mg/dL
  - HbA1c                  7.8 percent
  - Total cholesterol      226 mg/dL
  - LDL cholesterol        151 mg/dL
  - Troponin I             below detection threshold

A resting ECG showed normal sinus rhythm with no acute ST-segment
changes. A transthoracic echocardiogram was performed and reported an
ejection fraction of 52 percent with mild left ventricular hypertrophy.

A CT coronary angiogram demonstrated a focal stenosis of the proximal
left anterior descending artery measuring 65 percent luminal narrowing.
A second, non-obstructive lesion of 30 percent was noted in the right
coronary artery.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 - INTERVENTION (09 November 2024)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Following multidisciplinary review, the patient underwent invasive
coronary angiography. Intravascular ultrasound of the proximal LAD
lesion reclassified the stenosis at 78 percent, which is more severe
than the estimate obtained by CT.

A drug-eluting stent measuring 3.0 mm by 18 mm was deployed across the
lesion. Post-deployment imaging confirmed full expansion with no
residual stenosis and TIMI grade 3 flow. No periprocedural
complications were recorded.

The right coronary artery lesion was managed medically and no
intervention was performed at that site.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 - DISCHARGE (11 November 2024)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The patient was discharged in stable condition on the following
medications:

  - Aspirin 75 mg once daily
  - Ticagrelor 90 mg twice daily
  - Atorvastatin 40 mg once daily
  - Metformin 1000 mg twice daily
  - Ramipril 5 mg once daily

Dual antiplatelet therapy is to continue for twelve months. The
patient was enrolled in a supervised cardiac rehabilitation programme
commencing four weeks post-discharge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5 - FOLLOW-UP PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Cardiology review is scheduled at six weeks. Repeat lipid profile and
HbA1c are to be obtained at three months. The patient was counselled
on the importance of medication adherence, particularly the
antiplatelet regimen, and advised that premature discontinuation
carries a risk of stent thrombosis.

Reviewed and submitted by the attending cardiology team.
